Data & definitions

180-day completion

Denominator: positive FIT episodes with at least 180 days of observation by 6 Nov 2026. Numerator: colonoscopy completed 0 to 180 days after the positive result. Recent positives remain in active follow-up and are excluded from both numerator and denominator.

Workflow thresholds

Kit return: over 30 days. Missing referral: over 14 days after positivity. Navigator queue: over 90 days without colonoscopy. Escalation: over 180 days. Closure review: over 14 days after colonoscopy. These are configurable demo rules, not universal clinical deadlines.

Fictional patient data

5,000 reproducible synthetic episodes, seed 20261106. All are assumed average-risk, asymptomatic and due for screening. No names, contact details or real patient records. Group differences reflect simulation and sampling; they are not evidence about communities.

Real geographic context

CDC PLACES 2025 release, 2022 BRFSS-based model estimates, crude prevalence, ages 45 to 75. County screening coverage is not positive-FIT follow-up. The map is independent of patient filters and cannot validate the fictional cohort.

Completion is not closure

Closure requires colonoscopy evidence, pathology when a specimen was collected (otherwise N/A), a documented plan, patient acknowledgement, an owner and human review. Reminders and approvals never fabricate clinical events.

Prototype boundary

There is no patient-facing messaging, EHR connection, diagnosis or treatment recommendation. Session audit records are editable local demonstration data, not a clinical audit system. No clinical effectiveness or regulatory status is claimed.

This is a project-defined quality indicator, not a certified HEDIS implementation. A future pilot needs local pathway review, approved data access, consent, security controls and prospective evaluation.
